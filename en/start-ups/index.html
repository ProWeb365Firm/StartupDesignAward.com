LINY

LINY bikes

The LINY One is the first bicycle built for a sustainable circular economy.

LINY contributes to sustainable mobility in urban areas, because LINY isn't just a bicycle — it's a durable, reliable means of transportation that can be refurbished as part of a circular economy. That's the mission: to change the world, one bike at a time.

LEAF

LEAF – anti-aging for fruit and vegetables

LEAF is the best solution for keeping fruit and vegetables fresh longer in the fridge.

Many fruits and vegetables release ripening gas as they mature, which reduces the shelf life of food. Thanks to LEAF, this ripening gas is captured in the fridge, allowing fruit and vegetables to be stored together and stay fresh for longer. The vision: nothing less than putting an end to food waste.

Sneakprint

Sneakprint

Sneakprint brings the first custom-fitted designer sneaker to market, individualized using 3D-printing technology.

With Sneakprint, perfectly fitted sneakers finally make it into the shoe closet — comfortable to wear while responding to the individual needs of your feet. Sneakprint uses an impression kit (foam mold) to capture detailed information about the feet, which serves as the basis for producing a custom-made insole. The individual insole is manufactured using advanced 3D-printing technology. Sneakprint stands not only for high-quality materials, sustainability, and individuality, but also for an innovation that doesn't yet exist elsewhere on the market.

Winner 2022

myriad

With our autonomous gardens, we bring food production directly to the consumer.

The Myriad garden provides plants with everything they need for optimal growth: light, water, and nutrients. As a result, plants grow twice as fast as in a conventional garden. To grow your own, our seedlings are simply plugged into the garden, and water only needs to be topped up once a month. That means even inexperienced users can enjoy a bountiful harvest from the start, without lifting a finger.

urnfold

Through custom paper memorial urns, urnfold aims to find the ideal balance between contemporary design and sustainability.

After someone dies, a standardized and tightly scheduled process is often set in motion, leaving little room for the individual's needs. Values like ecological sustainability and the pursuit of individuality get little consideration in that process. At the same time, traditional, mostly religious rituals surrounding death are increasingly losing understanding and meaning. urnfold, however, is convinced that we need complementary, alternative rituals to be able to accept grief and death as part of life.

Monaco DUCKS

Our vision, make sustainability easy for everyone, leads us to our mission: build the world's first circular sneaker brand.

For our vision, we chose the product with the greatest possible impact: the sneaker. The lifestyle product of our generation, and the least sustainable product in the second-dirtiest market in the world — fashion. To bring a circular sneaker to market, we need not just the final product but also the infrastructure for post-consumer recycling. Together with strong partners from the industry, we're developing a solution here, becoming not just a sneaker brand but a solutions provider and a gamechanger for sustainable fashion.
