Taking part in the Start-up Design Award

Want to win the Start-up Design Award? Then apply now with your product! Your start-up must meet the following requirements:

  • The start-up has already been founded
  • At least a prototype must exist for the submitted product
  • The submitted product must not have been on the market for longer than 5 years
  • Start-ups with physical as well as digital products may apply

For detailed information on entering, please refer to the underlying Terms & Conditions. Do you meet all the criteria? Then we look forward to your application by 20 May 2023 via the application sheet.

Taking part in the Start-up Design Award is free of charge for all start-ups.

Evaluation criteria

A jury of renowned representatives from business and research puts your products through a thorough review.

The winners for the "Digital" and "Physical" categories are then chosen from the best start-up teams. Start-up Design Award winners may then display the winner logo and are honored with a cash prize. The following evaluation criteria are decisive:

Problem-solving

What individual benefit and utility does the product provide? Does the product/project solve a societal problem? What societal benefit does the product have?

Degree of innovation

How novel is the product? Is it a genuine innovation that didn't previously exist in this form?

Aesthetics & ergonomics

How appealing are the choice of materials, texture, finish, packaging, and graphic design? How ergonomic is the product?

Social & ecological impact

How sustainable is the product? Were recycled materials used? How high is the energy requirement? How durable is the product?

Functionality & usability

Does form follow function, or function follow form? Is the product intuitive to use? Is the product safe and accessible?

Emotional impact

Can the product's design create additional emotional value? Can the product, beyond its practical use, evoke satisfaction and joy?

About us

The Start-up Design Award is a competition run by the Institute for Entrepreneurship & Innovation at the University of Bayreuth.

The institute focuses on the central topics of innovative entrepreneurship. It combines its core competencies in the fields of research, teaching, and transfer. Through its work, the IEI passes on its innovation and founding expertise to the region and to industry. At the heart of the work is building founding and innovation skills, carrying out excellent, internationally visible basic and applied research, and sustainably strengthening the innovation and future capability of established companies as well as new business founders.

With the Start-up Design Award, the institute wants to reach out to young, innovative start-ups and give them a platform.
