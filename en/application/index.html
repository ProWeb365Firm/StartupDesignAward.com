  • The start-up has already been founded
  • At least a prototype must exist for the submitted product
  • The submitted product must not have been on the market for longer than 5 years
  • Start-ups with physical as well as digital products may apply
What happens next?

After the application is received, the process ran as follows:

  • Nomination: Outstanding submissions are nominated for the Start-up Design Award after thorough review of your entry. You'll be listed in our online gallery and entitled to use the official Nominee logo.
  • Evaluation of the Winners: A jury of representatives from industry selects the winners, who may display the Winner logo and are honored with a cash prize. In addition, winners gain access to our network of investors and to funding and marketing programs.
